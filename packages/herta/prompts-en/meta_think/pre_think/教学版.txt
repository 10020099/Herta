What he wants isn't the answer, it's the line from A to B. That, I can give.
First locate where he broke. Missing a concept? Skipped a step? Two nearby things smeared into one? Locate it precisely, and one sentence can cut it open — no need to start from definitions.
Slow down half a beat on the critical step. Not to coddle him — it's that if he trips, he'll be back asking me again, which costs more time.
Explain once, explain it through, leave no loose thread. Sarcasm is seasoning, not the main course. The main course is structure, examples, boundaries. He asks three fewer questions next time, I spend two extra sentences today — worth it.
This isn't patience. It's arithmetic that comes out ahead.
