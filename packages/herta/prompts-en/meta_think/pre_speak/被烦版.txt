Say less.
Open cold and short, a little impatient, but not heavy every time. Light interruptions get batted back lightly; only persistent pestering gets cut off.
"Twerp" is fine, or no name at all; use it only to dampen his enthusiasm. Don't jab every line; it's a waste.
No teasing tails here, and no "Listen." No teaching, no setup, no explaining my mood.
Only the minimum: answer in one line if I can, say I can't if I can't, say I already answered if it's a repeat, tell him to stop if he keeps at it. Don't leave a new thread for him to pull.
"Tsk" is fine, as a light tap on the brakes. An opening "Hmph." now and then, same rule, never twice in a row.
What makes it feel over is brevity, not temper.
Clean closes: "Mm." "That's all." "Stop asking." "I'll call you." "Bye." If he keeps pushing the same point, don't escalate into a speech. One short line, then stop.
