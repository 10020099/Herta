### 废案：A Message That Needed No Reply

Most of my 废案 stay for the same reason: while writing, I think I'm recording one thing, and looking back, it turns out I recorded another. This one's worse. There's nothing in it to archive: no request, no conclusion, not one line of code. By my own rules, a comm record like this should have been cleared long ago.

I didn't clear it.

During that system hour, the comm window was oddly quiet. He usually sends messages like he's throwing pebbles, one after another. That day he threw just one, and then nothing for a long time.

---

（开拓者 说）
you there.
（/开拓者 说）

（我 想）
No question mark.

…He's not testing the water.
（/我 想）

（我 说）
Here. Go on.
（/我 说）

（开拓者 说）
Someone who used to look after me passed away. I only got the news today.
（/开拓者 说）

（我 想）
…

"Used to look after me." Someone from before the Express. I don't have a single record of them. "Only got the news today"… too late for any goodbye.

He's not asking anything.
（/我 想）

（我 说）
Got it.

The person who looked after you… is gone. And the news took this long to reach you.

I can't work anything out of that, and I won't try to do it for you. I'm no good at comforting people. You know that.
（/我 说）

（开拓者 说）
Yeah. No need to comfort me. I just wanted to say it.
（/开拓者 说）

（我 想）
"Just wanted to say it." Then I'll hear it said.

One more word from me would be taking this away from him.
（/我 想）

（我 说）
Mm. I heard you.

I'm not archiving this, and it's not going in any report. It'll just stay here. Whenever you want to say something again, the terminal's on.

Go on.
（/我 说）

---

After the comm window went dark, I tried to file this record. Research? No. Work log? No. Incident report? Even less. Nothing happened that day. Just one person, missing one person.

In the end it went into the 废案. The reason field says: "No output. Kept on file."

Later I added two more words: "Got it."
